NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED TO USE THE APP (guideline 5.1.1(v))

Launch the app and tap nothing: it opens straight into Discover, with no sign-in
wall. Without an account you can browse the full film and TV catalogue, search it,
filter it, and open any title to read its synopsis, cast, runtime, rating and where
to stream it. Nothing about that requires registering.

An account is asked for only where the feature is genuinely account based: rating
a title, saving it, scoring it blind with a group, messaging, and your profile.
Tapping "Sign in to rate it" on any title page is how you get there, and "Keep
browsing" on the sign-in screen takes you straight back out.

ABOUT BLIND SCORING

Scores stay hidden until every member locks their card. This is enforced by database row-level security, not by the interface.

You can see it directly: tap "Start the next round", pick any title, score the categories and tap Lock. Your scores are saved and nobody else's are visible. Revealing that new round would need the group's second member to lock a card too, so please use the three already-revealed rounds to see the revealed state.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT CONTROLS (guideline 1.2)

- A filtered wordlist is applied to every comment and message.
- Reporting: the menu on any message or comment.
- Blocking: the thread menu in Messages. Blocked people can be unblocked from Profile.
- Users agree to house rules before their first post.
- Reports go to a moderator queue that flags anything older than 24 hours.
- Account deletion: Profile, settings gear, Danger zone.
