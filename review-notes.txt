1. GUIDELINE 1.2 - USER-GENERATED CONTENT
=====================================================================

All five required precautions are implemented. Here is exactly where to find
each one.

(a) TERMS / EULA WITH ZERO TOLERANCE, SHOWN BEFORE REGISTERING OR LOGGING IN
    Launch the app and tap the gold "Get started" button (top right). Before the sign-in form
    appears you get a full-screen agreement headed "Before you join", stating
    in bold that there is NO TOLERANCE FOR OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT OR ABUSIVE
    USERS, and that offending content is removed and the account ejected. You
    cannot reach the sign-in or create-account form without tapping "I agree
    to these rules".
    The agreement links to the full Terms of Use at
    https://mashpotato.app/terms, and the same agreement is restated under the
    button on the email sign-in/create-account form and password form.
    (In the previous build this agreement appeared before a user's first post
    rather than before registration. That is what changed.)

(b) FILTERING OF OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
    Every comment and message is checked against a blocked-term list on the
    server before it is stored; a match is refused and never posts. To see it,
    sign in, open any title, and post a take containing an obvious slur: the
    post is rejected.

(c) FLAGGING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
    Messages: More (bottom right) > Messages > open a conversation > tap any
    message bubble from the other person > "Report" in the sheet that opens.
    Comments: open any title > Discussion > tap the menu on any comment that
    is not yours > "Report".
    Content that draws three reports is hidden automatically while it waits
    for review.

(d) BLOCKING ABUSIVE USERS
    More > Messages > open a conversation > the menu in the top right >
    "Block". It is a two-step confirm.
    Blocking takes effect instantly: the person disappears from your view of
    the app, and they can no longer message you. Blocking ALSO notifies us -
    it files a report on that person's most recent message, which lands in the
    same moderator queue as any other report.
    Unblocking: Profile > settings gear > Blocked people.

(e) ACTING ON REPORTS WITHIN 24 HOURS
    Reports go to a moderator queue, ordered oldest first, which flags
    anything older than 24 hours. Acting on an item removes the content and
    can eject the account that posted it, and every action is written to an
    append-only audit log. The queue is reachable in-app for moderator
    accounts (More > Reports); it is not visible to ordinary users.

=====================================================================
2. GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) - NO ACCOUNT NEEDED FOR NON-ACCOUNT FEATURES
=====================================================================

Launch the app and tap nothing: it opens straight into Discover, with no
sign-in wall. Without an account you can browse the full film and TV
catalogue, search it, filter it, and open any title for its synopsis, cast,
runtime, rating and where to stream it.

An account is required only where the feature is genuinely account based:
rating, saving, scoring a title with a group, messaging, and your profile.
Tapping "Sign in to rate it" on a title page is how you get there, and "Keep
browsing" returns you to the catalogue.

=====================================================================
3. SIGNING IN TO REVIEW THE ACCOUNT FEATURES
=====================================================================

Tap "Get started", accept the terms, then "Sign in with a password instead".
Use the credentials above; no email code is required. Existing accounts
keep their normal landing page. New accounts set up their rubric and join
Test Group 1 automatically before entering the main app.

The account is in one group, "Movie Night", with three rounds already scored
and revealed, and it has a conversation containing a message from another
member so that reporting and blocking can be demonstrated immediately.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
1. The app opens on Home: the group, recent activity, and a saved list.
2. Tap Rate. The most recent round is revealed and shows the core of the app:
   one combined "Mashed" score, each member's individual score, and a headline
   naming the category the group agreed on and the one that split them.
3. Scroll for the per-category breakdown and an explanation of the weighting.
4. Tap any night in the group log to reopen that round's Reveal.

ABOUT BLIND SCORING
Scores stay hidden until every member locks their card. This is enforced by
database row-level security, not by the interface. You can verify it: tap
"Start the next round", pick a title, score it and tap Lock - your scores save
and nobody else's become visible. Revealing a NEW round needs the group's
second member to lock a card too, so please use the three already-revealed
rounds to see the revealed state.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile > settings gear > Danger zone.
